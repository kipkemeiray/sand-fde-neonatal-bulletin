5. Driver Analysis — and Why the Obvious Reading Is Wrong

The most important finding in this bulletin is a negative one. Unadjusted, facility capability appears to explain most of the mortality variation — CPAP availability alone correlates at -0.67. That reading is confounded by tier. Compare facilities only with their own peers and the associations collapse below |r| = 0.2, and 11 change sign entirely. This dataset does not support a causal intervention list. Acting on the unadjusted column would direct scarce capital at a spurious cause.

6. Overnight Staffing — the Group a Default CSV Read Deletes

63 of 117 facilities have no overnight staffing cover. In the source file this is recorded as the literal string None — a real category, not a missing value. Read with pandas' default settings it is silently converted to NaN, dropping the majority of the estate, and the worst-performing part of it, out of every grouped analysis. No error is raised. The pipeline disables default NA inference for exactly this reason.

Note the same caution as Section 5: night cover also tracks tier. The gradient is real and worth investigating; it is not on its own evidence that adding night staff would move mortality.
